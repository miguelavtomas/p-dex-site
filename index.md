# P-Dex

A retro card dex for your Pokémon TCG collection, for iPhone and the foldable iPhone.

- Scan cards with the camera, one at a time or a whole pile
- Every English set, with TCGplayer and Cardmarket prices
- Copies, conditions, notes, binder pages and a home-screen widget
- One-time purchase: no subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads, no tracking

Made in Portugal 🇵🇹
