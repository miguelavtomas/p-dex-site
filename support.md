# P-Dex Support

Questions, ideas, or a card that won't scan? Email **mavtomas@icloud.com**. Every message is read.

## Common questions

**A card isn't recognised.** Hold the card 10–15 cm from the camera, flat and well lit, with the collector number in view (bottom of the card). Tap the picture to focus, or use the torch in dim rooms. You can always add a card from the collection or by typing its number.

**The scanner offers several sets.** Some cards share a name and number across sets. Pick the one whose set symbol matches your card.

**Prices look different from the shop.** Prices are market guides from TCGplayer (USD) and Cardmarket (EUR), updated at most once a day. When the two markets disagree a lot, P-Dex shows a range.

**How do I move my collection to a new phone?** In the dex, open ⚙ OPTIONS → Export collection and save the file to iCloud Drive. On the new phone, use ⚙ OPTIONS → Restore from file.

**Is there a subscription?** No. P-Dex is a one-time purchase. It will never have a subscription or in-app purchases.
